# Code of conduct

Smart Parks Protect is built by and for people who protect wildlife and the people living around it. We keep this project a place where anyone can contribute.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct and honest, and be kind while doing it.
- Judge ideas, not people. Disagree with a proposal, not with the person who made it.
- Assume others act in good faith. Ask before concluding.
- Respect that contributors work in different time zones, languages and conditions, often in the field with poor connectivity.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks in any form.
- Publishing private information of others without permission.
- Deliberately disrupting discussions or reviews.

## Enforcement

Report problems to the maintainers through a private GitHub advisory or a direct message to a maintainer. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may block people who violate this code.

This code applies to the repository, issues, pull requests, documentation and any other channel used for the project.
